# src/data rules

- Discount codes live in `discountCodes` in src/data/pricing.ts and are applied client-side only on the enquiry page. `appliesTo: "website"` codes (MATESRATES) take their % off website one-off prices only, and a care plan adds a further 5% (Care) or 10% (Growth, Growth + AI), additive. `appliesTo: "all"` codes (LACUNA10, the welcome offer) take the same % off website one-off prices AND care-plan monthly fees, with no care-plan bonus. The discount line goes into the brief summary; codes are never shown in site copy, llms files or the services page bar.
- The welcome offer: src/components/services/DiscountPopup.tsx opens on /services only once the visitor has scrolled to the Choose-your-package section (the top of `#packages` within the upper 60% of the viewport, then a 600 ms pause; once per tab, not after an unlock, not for 14 days after a dismissal, not when a code is already entered); the email goes to Dave via the send-discount-unlock edge function, the unlock is kept in localStorage (src/lib/welcomeOffer.ts) and the enquiry page types the code into the discount field with a one-loop `.discount-sweep` ring (src/index.css), pre-filling it on later visits. The popup is always a dark card on MosaicBackdrop (src/components/services/MosaicBackdrop.tsx: React Bits Mosaic shader from src/components/react-bits/mosaic.tsx, lazy, behind canRunWebGLHero + prefers-reduced-motion, static pink glow + tile grid otherwise, dark gradient on top), black CTA with white text. The enquiry panel is the full package builder (website incl. Business pages, aftercare, code) and is the only place totals with discounts are shown; both groups ("1. Website", "2. Care plans") sit on ONE always-dark MosaicBackdrop (PANEL_GRADIENT) as glass OptionCards (kicker "Website · n of 3" / "Aftercare · n of 4", price, scope bullets, black Select / black-with-pink Selected; BusinessPagesSelect tone="dark" inside the Business card) and the cards are the only selectors: no pills and no (i) toggle; a grid on md+ (website md:grid-cols-3, care md:grid-cols-2 xl:grid-cols-4) and one embla carousel per group on phones (useIsMobile) that starts on the selected option — fieldsets there need min-w-0 or the carousel's min-content width overflows the panel.
- The enquiry form is a single step (Start your project: name, email and company required; phone and website optional) with no project questions; project type is derived silently from the chosen website. The page uses the same max-w-7xl column as the /services sections (form fields in two columns on md+); the package panel renders inline on desktop and as a sticky phone dock with a vaul Drawer editor; both share PackagePanel and the same state.
