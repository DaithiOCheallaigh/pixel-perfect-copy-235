# src/data rules

- Discount codes live in `discountCodes` in src/data/pricing.ts and are applied client-side only on the enquiry page. `appliesTo: "website"` codes (MATESRATES) take their % off website one-off prices only, and a care plan adds a further 5% (Care) or 10% (Growth, Growth + AI), additive. `appliesTo: "all"` codes (LACUNA10, the welcome offer) take the same % off website one-off prices AND care-plan monthly fees, with no care-plan bonus. The discount line goes into the brief summary; codes are never shown in site copy, llms files or the services page bar.
- The welcome offer: src/components/services/DiscountPopup.tsx opens on /services after 7 s or 35% scroll (once per tab, not after an unlock, not for 14 days after a dismissal, not when a code is already entered); the email goes to Dave via the send-discount-unlock edge function, the unlock is kept in localStorage (src/lib/welcomeOffer.ts) and the enquiry page types the code into the discount field with a one-loop `.discount-sweep` ring (src/index.css), pre-filling it on later visits. The popup is always a dark card: React Bits Mosaic shader (src/components/react-bits/mosaic.tsx, lazy, behind canRunWebGLHero + prefers-reduced-motion, static pink glow otherwise) under a dark gradient, black CTA with white text. The enquiry panel is the full package builder (website incl. Business pages, aftercare, code) and is the only place totals with discounts are shown; each group header has a small (i) DetailsToggle that expands TierDetails, an embla carousel with one card per option (scope bullets, price, Select) that starts on the selected option — fieldsets there need min-w-0 or the carousel's min-content width overflows the panel.
- The enquiry form is a single step (Start your project: name, email and company required; phone and website optional) with no project questions; project type is derived silently from the chosen website. The package panel renders inline on desktop and as a sticky phone dock with a vaul Drawer editor; both share PackagePanel and the same state.
