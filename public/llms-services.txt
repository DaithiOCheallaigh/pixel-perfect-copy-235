## Services & pricing (EUR, + VAT unless stated)

Prices + VAT at 23% unless shown as incl. VAT.
Free intro call and fixed written quote within 48 hours. Digital Health Check €250 (+€100 on site), half credited against custom work.

### Website tiers (one ladder for every business)

- Launch: €750 + VAT; or €70/mo + VAT × 12. Live in 7 working days.
- Website Refresh: from €1,500 + VAT; or €140/mo + VAT × 12. Live in 2–3 weeks from access to your current site.
- Business Site: €2,500 + VAT. Live in 3–4 weeks.
- Business Site pages: 5 pages €2,500, then €500 per extra page up to 10 (€5,000); blog from 6 pages, booking or CRM integration from 8, local SEO setup and 3 revision rounds at 10.
€150 per page beyond 5; new copy €100 a page. Sites over 10 pages or on an unsupported platform are quoted individually.
Need more? Bespoke builds from €7,500 + VAT, quoted in writing.
Industry pages (Kitchens & Joinery, Clinics & Practices, Salons & Barbers, Tours & Experiences) reuse these tiers.

### After launch

- I'll look after it myself: Free handover. At launch the site, hosting and domain go into accounts in your name; You pay the hosting and domain providers directly; 30-minute handover call and a short written guide; Edits when you need them, quoted per job based on the work.
- Lacuna looks after it: Care plans from €65/mo. Hosting, SSL, backups and uptime monitoring; Security updates and domain renewal; Email on your domain set up and managed (licences billed to you); A set amount of edit time every month.

### Care plans

- Care: €65/month. Hosting, SSL, backups, uptime monitoring; Security updates and .ie renewal; Email on your domain set up and managed (2 inboxes; licences billed to you); 30 minutes of edits a month; Reply within 1 business day.
- Growth: €200/month. Everything in Care; 60 minutes of edits a month; Review-request flow (asks every customer); Monthly report; One module for your industry.
- Growth + AI: €350/month. Everything in Growth; AI website assistant (up to 200 conversations, says it is an AI); Missed-call text-back; Quarterly 30-minute call.
3-month minimum for salons and restaurants, 6 months for others. Pay a year upfront, get 1 month free. Your domain, your content, your code. Export any time; no exit fee after the minimum term.
